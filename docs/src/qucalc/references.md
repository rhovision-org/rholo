# QuCalc — upstream references (quantum-os & the Quantum Logical Framework)

QuCalc is the rholo realization of two sibling projects, both now forked into the
Rho Vision org (`rhovision-org`) and linked throughout the QuCalc docs:

- [`rhovision-org/quantum-os`](https://github.com/rhovision-org/quantum-os) — the
  peer-to-peer **room**: a shared ZFA process space, capability-token identities, group
  decisions, and trust-governed **AI agent members**.
- [`rhovision-org/quantum-logical-framework`](https://github.com/rhovision-org/quantum-logical-framework) —
  the **foundation**: ZFA (Zero Free Action) as the security model, the 8-twist alphabet, and
  the machine-verified physics/logic substrate.

This page indexes the documents the QuCalc docs draw on, grouped by the three capabilities
QuCalc brings to rholo: **native AI**, **agent support**, and **decision support**. Links
point at the `main` branch of the forks.
