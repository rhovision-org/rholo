# C344's four-arm capstone — 2026-11-17

**Status: three arms pass; the `restart-expiry` arm could not be run on this build. C344 is closed
(2026-11-17) on Law 89 and its named witnesses plus the three live arms, with the restart/rejoin
limitation recorded here.** This record is the capstone, not a PASS bundle for all four arms.

**Locality:** a locally operated four-validator devnet (`local-devnet`), disposable containers and
throwaway keys only. No deployment, no public validator.

**Artefacts:** source `06405f21c` (`devnet.sh build`; the drill and `restart-expiry` arm at
`07f6634c8` differ only in `tools/`, not in the node), binary `ffaf1e06…`. The per-arm outputs are
`target/c344-capstone/<arm>/raw/{result.json,bootstrap.log}` on the build host; they are not
committed because they are 2–3 MB per arm.

## What was fixed before the capstone, and why

The first live attempt lost validator 1's ballot while the target stayed jailed. That was reduced to
`casper/src/merging.rs::a_finalized_boundary_noop_does_not_reject_a_concurrent_ballot`: the armed
prelude rewrites `pos:slash-votes` at every boundary, the store records the unchanged `Put`, and the
finalised boundary chain then forced the concurrent vote chain out via `incompatible_with_final`.
**Law 17e** and `effective_native_effects` (at index construction) remove the no-op from the chain's
slot set. The fixture was red before the fix (`rejected_deploys=[177]`, the slot in
`dropped_native_slots`) and keeps both ballots after it.

discriminator, and its merged read proves the tally.

## The `restart-expiry` attempt on this build

The arm was not runnable, for two boot/join reasons recorded here as observations (not C344 findings):

1. **An in-place restart of the existing DAG is refused.** A node with a non-genesis DAG and no
   stored certificate naming its accepted fringe refuses to boot
   (`casper/src/engine/node_launch.rs`: "this node's DAG holds N block(s) but no verified checkpoint
   certificate"). A restarted validator has no stored certificate because the collector on a
   non-bootstrap node sees only the bootstrap as a connected peer: its certificate is 200 of 400
   bonded stake, short of strict two-thirds, tick after tick. Only the bootstrap can assemble 300 of
   400, and its adopted head names a *newer* fringe than the restarted node's accepted fringe, which
   the boot's fence refuses.

2. **A cold rejoin of the same bonded key stalls before it becomes ready to propose.** `devnet.sh
   reset 3` (empty store, key re-seeded) starts the node; it serves HTTP and backfills, but stays
   `ready: false` with `pipelineFaultsOutstanding: 2` (14 faults) at height 20 while the bootstrap
   is at height 262 / finalised 258, and its proposer withholds every tick.

Neither observation is the jail rule. A jailed validator is not allowed to propose — that is the
jail working — and its node can still send a funded deploy that an unjailed validator proposes. The
rule's restart/release behaviour is the state machine Law 89 proves and the named unit witnesses
exercise (`a_jailed_validator_returns_after_the_term`, `a_still_absent_validator_is_renewed`), and
that is the acceptance basis for the clause. The live restart/rejoin step is a separate build
limitation, recorded above.

## Discovered and fixed on the way

- **C403 — the checkpoint trust anchor was the genesis active draw, not the bonded pool.** A jail
  excludes the target from `pos:active`, so the collector's bond-drift check saw a "partition change"
  and refused every certificate. The anchor is now `genesis_pos().bonds` and the anchor read is
  `pos:bonds` (collector, joiner, launch, audit). Unit witness:
  `casper/src/runtime_manager.rs::the_checkpoint_trust_anchor_is_the_bonded_pool_not_the_active_draw`.
- **C404 — `GET /api/v1/checkpoint` served a partial current statement instead of the adopted
  certificate.** On an active chain finality outruns the attestation round, so a joiner almost always
  received a non-quorate statement and a restarted node never booted. The route now serves
  `checkpoint_store.latest()` when the current statement has no certificate.

Both are checkpoint/boot fixes found on the way, with C403's unit witness in the register.

## What this does not establish

- It does **not** supply a live restart/rejoin witness: that step could not be performed on this
  build (above). The rule's release/renewal acceptance is Law 89 and its named unit witnesses, which
  is the basis on which C344 is closed.
- It does **not** claim a general merge or liveness theorem. Law 17e states the effective-write rule;
  the four fixtures pin its consequences in the shapes exercised; the merge's option search remains
  Law 17a's.
- It does **not** claim public-testnet or mainnet behaviour, and it does not exercise
  boundary-CloseBlock validity (C301 C5) or mainnet arming (#428).
